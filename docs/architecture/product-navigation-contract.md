# Product Navigation Contract

## Purpose

P1 Product Recovery 10 presents the existing AeroAssist Product Kernel as a
task-oriented product. Navigation is a projection over
`frontend/src/lib/moduleCatalog.js`; it does not grant permission, change a
canonical route, or create business state.

Backend authorization remains final. Hidden navigation is never an
authorization control.

## Platform Navigation

The Platform Console has nine primary areas, in this order:

1. Overview
2. Reference Data
3. Airline Knowledge
4. Policies
5. Agencies
6. Users
7. Monitoring
8. Audit
9. Settings

Specialist governance, diagnostics, lifecycle foundations, and catalogue
utilities remain reachable under a collapsed **Advanced** area. Platform role
filtering is applied before either primary or Advanced links are rendered.

## Agency Navigation

The Agency Workspace has fourteen primary areas, in this order:

1. Dashboard
2. Requests
3. Offers
4. Trips
5. Bookings
6. Tickets & EMDs
7. Finance
8. Clients
9. Passengers
10. Communications
11. Documents
12. Operations
13. Reports
14. Settings

Specialist tools remain under collapsed **Advanced** navigation. Settings is
limited to Agency owners and administrators. Permission and entitlement
filtering remains server-backed through the current authorization context.

## Portal Navigation

The Client Portal presents Dashboard, Trips, Offers, Requests, Documents,
Messages, Payments, and Profile. The Passenger Portal remains a distinct,
narrower projection and does not inherit Client-wide Requests, Payments, or
relationship access. This separation is a security boundary, not a cosmetic
variation.

## Search And Actions

Platform and Agency shells provide a permitted-page search over the same
navigation projection. Agency quick actions link to canonical request, offer,
trip, booking, document, and communication surfaces only when the current
membership has the required permission. These shortcuts do not execute
providers, bookings, tickets, payments, or external messages.

## Route Compatibility

All existing routes and valid deep links remain available. The primary
navigation intentionally uses one route per task area. Current compatibility
aliases include:

- `/agency` and `/agency/operations-command-center`
- `/platform/monitoring` and `/platform/pilot-operations`
- `/portal/travel-options` and `/portal/offers`
- `/platform/airline-knowledge-governance` and
  `/platform/airline-knowledge-releases`
- `/platform/document-templates` and `/platform/documents`

Aliases share an existing page component and do not create parallel business
ownership.

## Page Inventory

The governed inventory is
[`product-page-inventory.csv`](product-page-inventory.csv). It is generated by
`node frontend/scripts/audit-product-pages.mjs` and checked with `--check`.

The current inventory contains 311 page files:

- 117 Platform pages
- 156 Agency pages
- 34 Client or Passenger Portal pages
- 2 authentication pages
- 2 public pages

Placement classification:

- 30 primary task surfaces
- 69 contextual or detail surfaces
- 205 Advanced specialist surfaces
- 7 currently orphaned or unused pages

The orphaned pages are `AgencyDashboardPage`, `BookingCreatePage`,
`ClientsPage`, `OfferDetailPage`, `OffersPage`, `PassengersPage`, and
`PortalOffersPage`. They are excluded from navigation and recorded for a
separate evidence-led removal decision; this recovery does not delete them.

## Change Rules

- Add a primary area only for a distinct user task.
- Reuse canonical routes and domain owners.
- Keep technical diagnostics and implementation-era foundations out of normal
  navigation.
- Preserve role, membership, tenant, and portal boundaries.
- Register each page in `App.jsx` or deliberately record it as orphaned.
- Regenerate and review the page inventory after route or page changes.
